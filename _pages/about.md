I am a Master's student in Computational Linguistics and Informatics at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universitat Munchen. My work focuses on evaluating and interpreting foundation model behavior beyond single accuracy numbers.

My research interests include large language models, reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), multilingual and pragmatic reasoning, and human label variation (HLV). I am especially interested in how reasoning traces transfer across models and how evaluation frameworks can characterize reliability, robustness, and behavior across tasks.

Currently, I am a Scientific Programmer at Munich AI & NLP (MaiNLP), University of Munich (LMU), where I am developing an open-source, research-oriented toolkit for HLV. I also work on empirical evaluation of RAG metrics and on reasoning-transfer questions around chain-of-thought traces.
