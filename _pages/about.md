I am a Master's student in Computational Linguistics and Informatics at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universitat Munchen. My work focuses on evaluating and interpreting foundation model behavior beyond single accuracy numbers.

My research interests include large language models, reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), multilingual and pragmatic reasoning, and human label variation (HLV). Selected papers related to these interests include [Reasoning That Travels: Dissecting How Chain-of-Thought Transfers Across Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28913) and [XToM: Exploring the Multilingual Theory of Mind for Large Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.805/).

Currently, I am a Scientific Programmer at Munich AI & NLP (MaiNLP), University of Munich (LMU), where I am developing an open-source, research-oriented toolkit for HLV.
